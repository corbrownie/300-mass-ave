02 / INDUSTRY

Named partners — NVIDIA, Microsoft, QBase and others — embed their latest technology into the building as a live, continuously evolving environment. Faculty research, student co-ops, and partner R&D flow in both directions.

Six Firsts

Six features no other university hub offers.

01

QBase Quantum Hub + Installation

EdenBase's $100M quantum fund operates from the Startup Hub. A kinetic data sculpture anchors the lobby. The only university hub in North America with active quantum capital in residence from Day 1.

02

Northeastern Network Wall

A live, always-on lobby display showing real-time activity across the 14-campus network: founders, events, capital raised, cross-hub connections. No other university has a live physical demonstration of a global founder network.

03

AI Entrepreneurship Commons

An open-floor intelligent working environment: AI co-creation stations, multi-screen walls, projection mapping, spatial audio. No Boston university hub has an AI-native open working environment as its ground floor.

04

Soft Landing Programme

Any Northeastern founder, resident, alumni, or student can request a desk in any hub city within 48 hours at no additional cost. Membership becomes a global product — not a Boston workspace.

05

Intelligent Career Futures Hub

AI career matching, VR interview simulation, and a startup talent marketplace — co-located with the startup community students are matched to. Physical co-location of career services with resident startups is unique in Boston.

06

Library Futures Forum

A historic double-height library with projection mapping, spatial audio, and dynamic lighting — the most architecturally and technologically distinctive event venue in Boston's university landscape.

300 Mass Ave

A Historic Landmark, Reimagined.

Horticultural Hall — English Renaissance Revival, built 1901, opposite Symphony Hall — is one of Boston's most architecturally significant buildings.

The contrast between its historic architecture and the intelligent systems layer installed within it is not a compromise. It is the defining experience of the Centre: an institution confident enough in its future to house it in a building that has already stood for 125 years.
